# NIMORAI Multilingual Content Strategy

## Current catalog reality

The 600-topic inventory presently contains **594 English-title rows and 6 Bengali-title rows**, not 600 fully translated pairs. Many English topics are marked as Bengali localization candidates. A translated article is a language variant of its underlying topic, **not another unique topic ID**.

## Language priorities

1. Global English, with neutral, simple technical explanations and current sources.
2. Native, technically edited Bangla for high-value foundational and troubleshooting topics.
3. Hindi and other languages only after editorial capacity and measured audience demand justify them.

## Workflow

- Select priority English article, confirm reader intent in the target language via real query research.
- Use a native subject-matter editor to **transcreate**, not blindly word-swap, examples and terminology.
- Retain code identifiers, blockchain/network standards and important English terms where local readers expect them.
- Adapt FAQs and examples to local questions without inventing region-specific legal or payment support.
- Publish in /blog/bn/… with a human-reviewed transliterated/descriptive slug.
- Add reciprocal hreflang when both live language versions are accessible.
- Track impressions/clicks and improve Bangla terminology on actual query evidence.

## Bangladesh safeguards

Any discussion of local law, crypto payments, taxes or regulatory restrictions needs a date, authoritative country-specific sources and legal review when appropriate. Avoid definitive legality claims or instructions for circumventing restrictions. Educational content is not a solicitation to buy crypto assets.
