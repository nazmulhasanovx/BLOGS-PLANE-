# NIMORAI Internal 100-Point SEO Editorial QA Score

This is a **proposed internal acceptance rubric**, not Google's ranking formula, a Google score, or a promise of #1 search position. A draft scoring 100 here may still rank poorly or not be indexed.

| Area | Max | Pass criteria |
|---|---:|---|
| Search intent fit | 15 | Directly answers target question; not a duplicate of sibling topics; live SERP checked |
| Factual accuracy / primary evidence | 15 | Technical claims, figures, standards, pricing and availability checked and cited |
| Original usefulness | 15 | Original examples, diagrams, test results, decision logic, or practical insights |
| Readability and user experience | 10 | Clear opening answer, correct headings, actionable explanation, accessible reading flow |
| On-page SEO | 10 | Unique H1/title/snippet, relevant terms naturally, descriptive URL, image alt text |
| Internal linking / topical context | 10 | Pillar and genuinely relevant articles linked with descriptive anchors and no orphans |
| Technical indexability | 10 | HTTP 200, crawlable/indexable, canonical correct, sitemap entry, working links |
| Performance and accessibility | 5 | Responsive mobile, fast usable page, alt text, contrast, keyboard support |
| Structured data / locale | 5 | Valid appropriate Article/BlogPosting where used; correct hreflang only for actual translations |
| Trust, disclosure and governance | 5 | Accurate author/reviewer, date, product status, scope, update policy and ethics |
| **Total** | **100** | |

### Thresholds

- 90–100: Editorial release candidate **only if all hard gates pass**.
- 80–89: Revision required.
- Below 80: Major revision or rethink topic.
- Hard failures override score: unsupported NIMORAI product promise; false payment confirmation; copied material; fabricated pricing/metrics; unsupported legal conclusion; materially unsafe advice; missing required citations; generated low-value duplicate page.

### Pre-publication hard gates

[ ] Unique search intent validated  
[ ] Technical reviewer signed off if needed  
[ ] Competitive/primary sources verified  
[ ] Content includes genuine unique reader value  
[ ] No fake NIMORAI product availability or deployment claim  
[ ] Title, H1, canonical and requested language checked  
[ ] Link targets and sitemap valid  
[ ] Editor approved; preview checked on desktop and mobile  
[ ] Article author, reviewer and updated date present  
[ ] Evidence-based final QA score documented

### Post-publication QA

Check indexed/canonical status, actual search impressions and user engagement after search data accrues; update or consolidate based on evidence. Do not infer poor quality merely from a short period without impressions.
