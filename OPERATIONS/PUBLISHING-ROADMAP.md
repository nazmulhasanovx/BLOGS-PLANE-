# 24-Month Editorial Roadmap (Capacity Proposal)

**Candidate window:** Dec 2026 through Nov 2028.
**Total:** 600 planned topics = 25 candidate monthly slots.

This is a backlog allocation, not a promise of 600 finished posts by a deadline. Quality and search evidence can reduce, merge, defer or reorder releases.

## Four six-month waves

| Wave | Candidate topics | Primary purpose | Gates |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Months 1–6 | 150 | Build foundational education, publish first cluster hubs, learn from GSC | Good original evidence; no thin posts |
| Months 7–12 | 150 | Deepen developer and creator workflows, test comparison usefulness | No contradictory claims; verify competitor facts |
| Months 13–18 | 150 | Expand long-tail supporting articles and native Bangla | Multilingual QA and real audience insights |
| Months 19–24 | 150 | Fill remaining gaps, consolidate overlaps, refresh winners | Reassess P2 intent and opportunity before production |

Each month pulls the top available topics by round-robin cluster order, with all P0 planned before P1, then P2. Blocked NIMORAI product guides must be replaced with eligible topics rather than auto-released.

## Editorial pilot

Begin with a quality pilot (e.g. 8–12 approved posts) and measure operational capacity before attempting 25/month. Record median writing/review time, rejected draft rate, live indexability and reader usefulness. Reforecast all remaining slots when those measures are known.

## Quarterly review

Recheck product availability, content overlap, crawl issues, SERP competitors and topic demand. Refresh winners, prune redundant low-value pages and avoid publishing merely to hit the 600-count milestone.
