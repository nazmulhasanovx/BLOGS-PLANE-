# Content Release Gates

1. Topic ID exists and is unique in data/master-600.csv.
2. Target keyword and live search intent independently reviewed.
3. Article is sufficiently differentiated from sibling articles and competitor pages.
4. First-party sources collected; dates and technical constraints stated accurately.
5. NIMORAI-specific claims tied to actual deployment evidence.
6. English or Bangla reviewer approved final user-facing copy.
7. Original diagrams/screenshots are authorized and accessible.
8. Internal links use live destinations; no fake article links.
9. Technical metadata, canonical, sitemap, structured data and hreflang verified.
10. SEO rubric recorded and all blockers resolved.
11. A human explicitly approves CMS publication.
12. Post-publication URL/status entered into editorial records.

Red flags: unsupported finance/legal claims, invented on-chain states, copied source text, guaranteed rank, scaled duplicate content, fake reviews, forced backlink manipulation, empty translation pages.
