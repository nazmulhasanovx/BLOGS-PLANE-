# NIMORAI 600 Blog — Master SEO Growth Plan

**As of 2026-11-19.** This is an editorial roadmap, not a report of achieved SEO outcomes.

## Product positioning

NIMORAI is planned as a Web3 creation and engineering platform. Its **first customer scope** centers on Easy Token Builder, Easy NFT Builder, Easy Coin Builder, shared billing/payment verification and managed order tracking. Advanced IDE, API, Chain, wallet and other ecosystem features are future or conditional modules. The blog must clearly separate general education from **actually verified** product capability.

## Search goals

1. Earn topical credibility in basic blockchain, tokens, NFTs, networks and smart contracts.
2. Reach searchers who are comparing no-code builders and managed development services.
3. Answer operational concerns other guides often under-explain: token delivery evidence, payment states, mismatched networks, refunds, wallet visibility, QA and transparent invoices.
4. Publish neutral first-party-evidence comparisons against relevant tools without misleading users.
5. Serve global English readers while building an expert-reviewed Bangla knowledge base for Bangladesh / South Asian audiences.
6. Make helpful, citeable explanations that may be understood by search engines and AI assistants, rather than mechanically optimizing for mentions.
7. Convert informed visitors to relevant NIMORAI services **only when the feature really exists and is enabled**.

## Priority model

- **P0 (120):** six high-value topics per cluster, written only after SERP and technical review.
- **P1 (240):** twelve supporting topics per cluster, scheduled after P0 hubs establish coverage.
- **P2 (240):** deeper long-tail and reference topics.
- The candidate calendar spreads clusters round-robin rather than publishing 30 similar articles together.
- **Do not publish a P0 branded guide before its feature is verified.** Replace its calendar slot with another approved topic, keeping the topic ID.
- No reported traffic/search volume/difficulty numbers until measured.

## Editorial content types

- Evergreen explainers: clear, direct definitions with illustrations and boundaries.
- How-to articles: prerequisites, steps, validation, exception handling, outcomes.
- Comparison articles: neutral scope, date-stamped features, honest trade-offs.
- Pricing articles: itemized verified quotes; no hypothetical amounts sold as live prices.
- Reference pages: glossaries, checklists, architecture diagrams and templates.
- Product documentation: screenshots and procedures matching the current deployed product.

## Conversion mapping

- Early-funnel learning -> NIMORAI Guide / learning hub.
- Token intent -> Easy Token Builder only when live and compatible.
- NFT intent -> Easy NFT Builder only when live.
- Coin planning -> Coin Application only when actual policy and scope confirmed.
- Developer API -> docs only for available endpoints.
- Billing/verification topics -> official help / accurate order policy, not pressured repurchase.
- Comparisons -> neutral decision guide before any CTA.

## 24-month candidate editorial capacity

25 **candidate** posts per calendar month, Dec 2026 to Nov 2028 = 600 proposed slots. This is an editorial queue, NOT auto-publish dates. Start with a quality-controlled pilot, measure production capacity and reforecast monthly. Suggested phase gates:
- Months 1–3: publish a smaller approved pilot, measure indexing/engagement.
- Months 4–6: expand only where reviewers can sustain quality.
- Months 7–12: prioritize winning clusters, improve linking, refresh stale pages.
- Months 13–24: continue dependent on outcomes; consolidate underperforming or overlapping topics.

## KPIs to collect (no fabricated baselines)

Google Search Console: indexed canonical URLs, impressions, clicks, average position by query, eligible CTR by URL, branded vs nonbranded queries.
Analytics: organic engaged sessions, meaningful feature-entry clicks, lead / account conversions, assisted conversions.
Content governance: qualified posts published, audit failures, refresh age, sourced original diagrams, intent cannibalization cases.
Technical: crawlability, sitemap coverage, page performance, structured-data validity and responsive usability.

## SEO and safety policy

Google guidance prioritizes helpful people-first content and identifies scaled low-value publication as spam-risk. A list of 600 titles is useful **only** if each resulting article genuinely earns a separate place in search results. Merge or cancel posts with identical SERP intent.
