# NIMORAI — 600-Article SEO Editorial Roadmap

**Branch:** nimorai | **Version:** 1.0 | **Prepared:** 2026-11-19 | **Owner:** NIMORAI / NEONECY

This branch is the **NIMORAI-only editorial planning workspace** inside the shared BLOGS-PLANE- repository. It is NOT a published blog and is NOT a guarantee of SEO ranking.

## Confirmed inventory

- **600 unique planned topics** (NIM-001 … NIM-600).
- **20 topic clusters**, each with 30 titles.
- **600 individual Markdown editorial briefs**, each with metadata, suggested structure, evidence requirements and publication gate.
- **600 master CSV rows**: data/master-600.csv.
- **24-month candidate calendar**: data/24-month-editorial-calendar.csv, 25 candidates per month (Dec 2026–Nov 2028).
- **Priorities:** P0 = 120, P1 = 240, P2 = 240.
- **Languages as presently authored:** 594 English working titles, 6 Bengali working titles; English-first with human-edited Bengali localization of high-priority content.
- All 600 items are **planned/unpublished**, not finished blog articles.

## Where to start

1. Read MASTER-PLAN.md for business goals, audiences, priority sequencing and scope.
2. Read SEO/COMPETITOR-RESEARCH.md for first-party competitor benchmarks and topic-gap hypotheses.
3. Review data/master-600.csv and filter by P0 and candidate_month.
4. Open the matching briefs/NN-cluster/NIM-XXX.md file and conduct live SERP / keyword validation.
5. Follow OPERATIONS/EDITORIAL-WORKFLOW.md, SEO/SEO-100-POINT-QA.md and the publishing gates.
6. Approve the article as a human editor before releasing to the website.

## Safeguards

- Search volume, keyword difficulty, live rankings and competitor positions are **not measured** in this plan.
- A high internal QA score does **not** equal a guaranteed Google rank or Lighthouse score.
- Product feature claims must match actual deployed capabilities; NIMORAI-branded topics NIM-571–NIM-600 are blocked until verified.
- Avoid scaled low-value content, investment return promises, copied competitor copy, invented demos and claims of verified payments without chain/provider checks.
- The topic plan intentionally makes **20 topic hub pages possible**, but they are navigation hubs and not counted as finished articles.

For updates, preserve article IDs and record any merges, deletions or intent consolidation; do not silently renumber topics.
